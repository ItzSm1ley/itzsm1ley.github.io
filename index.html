Welcome to TIMUN LPA

TIMUN LPA is a high school level Model United Nations club based at Lycée Pilote de l’Ariana and part of the broader TIMUN Clubs network in Tunisia.

Learn More

What is TIMUN?

Tunisian International Model United Nations (TIMUN) is a simulation of the United Nations where people take on the roles of delegates representing various countries. These delegates debate, negotiate, and draft resolutions on global issues, mirroring the real-world UN.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the club?

Wait for the dedicated application periods (typically at the start of September and January) and apply, we will contact you when accepted.

Do I need experience in politics to join?

No as long as you are curious enough about global geopolitics.

What do I gain by joining?

You gain experience in public speaking as MUNs force you to defend opinions that are not necessarily yours, you may also gain experience in media, finance, and academics.
